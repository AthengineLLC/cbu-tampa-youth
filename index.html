CBU Season Insurance — Optional

$125 9U–12U  ·  $150 13U–14U

Protect your investment against a player injury. Purchase CBU Season Insurance and, with proof of a doctor's note, your family receives a prorated refund based on the date of injury.

  • Refunds are prorated by date of injury — if 40% of the season is missed, you get 40% of the season cost back.
  • If a player is injured before the season starts, you receive a full refund of the season cost. The uniform and the insurance cost itself are non-refundable.

Why CBU

The CBU Difference

Three things that set our players up to keep playing after high school.

1

College-First Coaching

An experienced staff that coaches to the standard college programs recruit to — preparing youth players for high school and beyond.

2

Real Evaluation

Every player is evaluated honestly and tracked over time, so families always know exactly where they stand.

3

Year-Round Development

Skill work, metric tracking, and competitive play across the calendar — not just a short summer window.

Built In-House

A development system for coaches and players

We've built a complete evaluation and player-development system for CBU — coach and director evaluations, AI scouting cards, and college-contact tracking — designed to support both our coaches and our players at every step of their journey.

New for Fall 2026

Metric Tracking

We'll host multiple Metric Days throughout the season to track the key metrics we believe in for player development — giving every player real, measurable benchmarks to work toward and chart over time. New to our Fall 2026 season.
